## A. Broken now, or a wrong answer with no warning

**A1. The window cannot start an S-parameter run.** ✔ `[gui] S · no · verified`
- `app.py:1494` `self.start = StartPage(self)` (6828ec1, 2026-11-13) hides `def start(self, preview)` at `:2816`.
  `_start_job` (`:1799`) and the automatic preview (`:2746`) then call a QWidget: `TypeError: 'StartPage' object
  is not callable`.
- `_next_job` has already set `self.job = 'rf'`, so `busy()` stays True and `stop()` returns early. Every later Run
  says "A run is going" until a restart.
- The tests miss it because `tests/test_study.py:260` monkeypatches `_start_job`.
- Fix: rename the attribute and make `_next_job` exception-safe. The same stuck state follows a non-ValueError from a
  plugin's `command`, or an OSError from `Run()`. Add one test that drives `w.preview()` against a stand-in run
  script.

**A2. Thermal is handed a coupled winding's transmitted power as heat.** ✔ `[pcbqs] S · no · verified`
- `pcbqs/src/pcbqs/thermal/problem.py:185-201` sums each part's `power_W` per footprint. A coupled winding's
  `power_W` is what it passes on, which `docs/circuit.md:97` itself says is not a loss.
- Probe: with PRIMARY on footprint L5, L5 gets 10.47 W for a pair that loses 0.45 W. A secondary on the board goes
  negative and is dropped as "no loss".
- Fix: measure each winding's own i²R (its series R and core R) in the deck and hand that over. At least, hand over a
  coupled group's sum only when all its windings share one footprint.

**A3. Gate-drive power has the wrong sign in the loss table and reaches no part.** ✔ `[pcbqs] S · no · verified`
- `pcbqs/src/pcbqs/reports.py:354-355` appends `-gate_drive_power_W`, while `engine.py:146` has
  `loss = source + gate - load`.
- On wpthb the rows sum to 0.7139 W against `loss_W` 0.7318 W (97.6 %).
- The dissipation in `RG_` and the driver is in no footprint's loss for thermal.
- The only test (`tests/test_analyses.py:281-294`) uses `rc_filter`, which has no gates.

**A4. The line model drops ground vias, so shorted stubs are open stubs.** ✔ `[kicad2ems lines] S · no · verified`
- `kicad2ems/src/kicad2ems/lines/builder.py:134-135` keeps only vias on the line's own nets. dfsynth draws every short
  as a GND via (`dfsynth/src/dfsynth/kicad.py:101-103`).
- dfsynth's stub band-pass comes out as a band-stop on the line model: −80 dB at 3 GHz, where dfsynth's own model
  gives −0.4 dB. The interdigital peaks at −47 dB against −1.5 dB.
- `dfsynth --validate lines` and `validate_all.sh` feed these back without a note.
- Fix: an other-net via whose pad overlaps a line end becomes a shunt to its net (`via_inductance`, or MVIA).

**A5. The window's Stop doesn't stop the tools' own children.** `[gui, fem, pcbqs] S · no · verified (probe)`
- `kicad2ems-gui/src/kicad2ems_gui/analysispanel.py:598-600` SIGKILLs the direct child only. pcbqs's SIGTERM handler,
  which stops ngspice (`pcbqs/src/pcbqs/circuit/__main__.py:66-70`), never runs.
- kicad2ems-fem starts ElmerSolver and Palace/mpirun/podman (`elmer/run.py:64`, `palace/run.py:72`) with no process
  group and no stop check, so Palace runs on. The README gives 30 min and 19 GB for demo_lowpass.
- Stop on a job with several rails doesn't clear `_queue`, so the next rail starts at once.
- Fix:
  - terminate, then kill after about 3 s;
  - start each tool with `start_new_session` and signal the group;
  - have the fem tools watch `KICAD2EMS_STOP` and `killpg`;
  - clear the queue on Stop.

**A6. mom25d solves a log sweep on a linear grid.** ✔ `[mom25d] S · no · verified (probe)`
- `mom25d/src/mom25d/afs.py:94-96` seeds and checks convergence on `linspace`. `api.py:88` then `np.interp`s onto
  the requested log points, and `kicad2ems/mixed.py:321` does the same.
- Probe on a 100 kHz pole over 10 Hz–1 GHz: 7 solves, one below 1 MHz, max |ΔS| 1.79, 82 of 201 points off by more
  than 0.05.
- That is the band the loop/tree basis and the pcbqs join are for, so the join's crossover is distorted too.
- Fix: seed and gap-split in log f, and evaluate the AAA model at the requested points.

**A7. Blind and micro vias keep only their two end layers.** ✔ `[kicad2ems import] S · no · verified (probe)`
- `kicad2ems/src/kicad2ems/importers/kicad/board.py:119-122` expands only `through` vias across the stack.
- The antipad test in `board.py:316,333` therefore misses the layers between. In the probe, `--fill-voids all`
  filled an In1.Cu antipad around a blind via F.Cu→In2.Cu, shorting the signal via to the plane. `far` did the same
  once the antipad was under 0.5 mm².

**A8. Crosstalk that was never computed is reported as 0 % and "limit met".** ✔ `[si] S · no · verified (probe)`

  says so in a comment). `kicad2ems/src/kicad2ems/si.py:385-390` reads them back as 0, and `worst()` (`:422-432`)
  says "met".
- Broadside pairs on adjacent layers pass on no information.
- Fix: `ok=None`, "not modelled: check with a full-wave solver".

**A9. The crosstalk search proposes ground and power nets.** ✔ `[si] S · no · verified`

  tuples (`importers/kicad/board.py:133-134`), so `poured` is always empty.

**A13. kicad2ems-fem's coil proposal is a wpthb special case.** ✔ `[fem] S · no · verified`

**A14. PDN: isolated plane domains are written as impedances, and mounts have no resistance.** `[pcbqs pdn] S–M · no · verified`

**A17. Copper and dielectric loss on the line model.** `[lines] S–M · no · verified (probe)`

  field-solved section (`fastsolve.py:100-108,342`, `coupled.py:120-145`). The plugin still declares
  `conductor_loss='yes'`, and `docs/transmission-lines.md:329` says `--sigma` adds copper loss.
- Dielectric loss uses the worst layer's tan δ (`reference.py:15-24`), while the field solver's G is computed and

  The single εr also forces stripline FEXT to 0.
- There is no frequency-dependent Dk/Df (no Djordjevic-Sarkar).

- Fix: generate vdmos switches with Cgs/Cgd/Cds, as wpthb's model has, or add the overlap after the run from
  Qgd/Rg. Either way, the report should say which switches have ideal edges.

## D. Performance (measured gains first)

**Circuit**
- **D1. Knob runs repeat 200 periods after the first run has settled.** `[pcbqs] S · partly (Correctness 4)`
  - `engine.py:521`. wpthb with `cycles=50` takes 26.5 s instead of 40.9 s, with the same answer to 5 digits.
  - Fix: start knob runs at `cycles // 4` and double only when a run hasn't settled.
- **D2. Each trial's waveforms are written as text, read, compressed, then deleted.** `[pcbqs] S · partly`
  - Each wpthb run writes 98 MB of text. `loadtxt` takes 0.55 s and `savez_compressed` 0.55 s, on files that are
    deleted. With copper, the spectrum is computed twice.
  - That is about 1 s of each 9.7 s run. Fix: binary raw (`read_raw` exists), and compress only the accepted run.
- **D3. `--fields` re-meshes and re-solves copper the circuit already has.** `[pcbqs] M · no`
  - Buck at 4 A: 37.6 s of 62 s. Fix: reuse `prepare()`'s MQS solve and currents.

**FDTD**
- **D4. Every excitation rebuilds the operator and the GPU context.** `[gfdtd, fdtd] M`
  - That is 8–51 % of a GPU run on the examples. Only the excitation table differs between ports.
- **D5. The 10 µm solder mask sets the global time step.** `[fdtd] S, then M`
  - `nz_mask` defaults to 4 cells: the hairpin ran 17× longer, with results "within 1 MHz".
  - Default to 1, then add sub-cell εr averaging.
- **D6. The time step is up to 15 % shorter than openEMS's on the same mesh.** `[gfdtd] S`
  - It is a global worst-case sum (`operator.py:212-221`); hairpin 105.9 fs against 124.9 fs.
  - Fix: a node-local criterion, as openEMS's method 3.
- **D7. Host memory is about 470 B/node at build, 16× the GPU's, and there is no VRAM check.** `[gfdtd] M`
- **D8. The energy check syncs the GPU every 100 steps, PortWatch polls at openEMS's pace, and dumps block the loop.** `[gfdtd, fdtd] S–M`
- **D9. Excitations are not batched, and there is no multi-GPU.** `[gfdtd] M–L`

**mom25d**
- **D10. Wave-port characterisation can cost more than the sweep.** `[mom25d] M`
  - On launch.json it builds a 19,698-unknown line for a 6,635-unknown board: 81 s, against 3 s for the board solve.
  - The line's planes are meshed at dmax, not the board's cells, and nothing is cached.
- **D11. The via and hole blocks are Python loops, rebuilt every frequency.** `[mom25d] M · partly`
  - 0.32 s of a 1.2 s solve. The plugin's own cost model gives about 76 s a solve for 1,000 vias.
  - The fill is never overlapped with the GPU factor.

**pcbqs fields**
- **D12. The PDN sweep factorises 2–3 times per frequency.** `[pdn] S`
  - CM5 +5V: 320 `gstrf` calls take 5.9 s of 22.3 s.
  - Fix: factor the bare matrix once with the part DOFs as right-hand sides. Loaded Z, transfer and decap what-ifs
    then come almost free.
  - `write_viewer` takes 6.3 s, through `json.dump`.
- **D13. Every frequency is a fresh dense complex128 LU.** `[pcbqs] M · partly`
  - With `dc` loss, the pencil `Rl + jωLl` allows one eigendecomposition plus Woodbury for the parts.
  - complex64 with refinement: 0.19 s against 0.38 s at n = 4000.
- **D14. The near-field CLI cannot select the compressed solver that exists.** `[pcbqs] S · roadmap stale`
  - `MQS(matrix_backend='hierarchical')` gives GMRES in the loop basis. The docs give 47,548 triangles in 13 s.
  - The roadmap's "3–5 days" for the whole-board near field overstates what is left.
- **D15. The sparams plugin solves 31 points and interpolates linearly in log f across resonances.** `[pcbqs] S–M`
  - Use mom25d's AAA.
- **D16. The dense via matrix is a Python double loop, and the loop tree is built in branch order.** `[pcbqs] S`
  - About 15 s at 2,500 segments. Mean loops are 35–45 branches; a BFS tree gives shorter ones.

**kicad2ems, the window and kicad2ems-fem**
- **D17. Quadratic loops in the line-model builder take about 5 s of the 11.6 s CM5 build (94 nets).** `[lines] S`
  - `_split_at_junctions` makes 1.28 M `distance` calls, plus a via generator and the pairing.
- **D18. `si.sections()` re-solves each pair's section, plus three widened copies.** `[si] S`
  - About 264 solves for a 12-net bus (inferred). Its broad `except` drops stretches silently.
- **D19. The lumped-part gap search unions a layer's copper once per part.** `[kicad2ems] S`
  - 0.61 s of a 0.95 s `Board` build. An STRtree fixes it.
- **D20. There is no result caching or resume, and solvers run one after another.** `[kicad2ems, fdtd] M`
- **D21. The 3D view recolours all 24 phases on the GUI thread on every control step.** `[gui] M`
  - 0.6 s at 128 voxels, 3.2 s and a 1.4 GB peak at 256.
- **D22. The GUI thread re-reads the run folders on every settings change.** `[gui] S–M`
  - Each call takes 20 ms at 200 runs, times the analyses and rails asked.
  - `prepare_example` and `dfsynth --describe` also run on the GUI thread.
- **D23. `elmer_dc` meshes tetrahedra where the layered wedge mesh already solves it.** `[fem] S–M`
  - 1.1 M tetrahedra at about 3.5 GB per million, against 40 s and 1.3 GB for the whole wpthb board on wedges.
  - The `.msh` is rewritten in Python, and `mag` runs n(n+1)/2 solves.

## E. Missing features

**Across the solvers**
- **E1. De-embedding and reference-plane shift.** `[kicad2ems, fdtd, mom25d] M`
  - Lumped ports aren't de-embedded (`docs/model.md`).
  - The FDTD model uses lumped ports only, although gfdtd has MSL, stripline and CPW ports.
  - mom25d's lumped ports carry their own via.
  - Ports and parts must lie along x or y.
- **E2. Passivity and causality.** `[all] S (warn) / M (enforce)`
  - Nothing checks them, and `sim.py`'s σmax is never called. A lossless two-port's AAA model reached σmax 1.0018.
  - AFS hitting its solve cap says nothing.
- **E3. Mesh convergence.** ✔
  - `--converge`'s code works through any engine, but it sits in the openEMS subclass, and gfdtd logs "not supported
    yet - ignored" (S: move it to the base).
  - mom25d has none, and demo.json's mesh has a 0.08° minimum angle (M).
  - Palace's adaptive refinement is unused (M).
- **E4. Materials.** `[kicad2ems] M`
  - KiCad's stackup `material` names are ignored, and defaults are silent.
  - There is no material library and no dispersion in the line model.
- **E5. Internal ports at part pads, then network embedding.** `[kicad2ems] L`
  - That gives instant value sweeps, vendor S-parameter parts and optimisation. Today every value change is a full
    re-solve, and there is no `[sweep]` driver.
- **E6. Board cutouts stay dielectric, and a region that splits the board keeps the largest piece silently.** `[kicad2ems] M`

**Power and thermal (within the circuit's purpose)**
- **E7. Junction temperature and limits.** `[pcbqs] S`
  - "Part °C" is the copper under the pads, theta comes only from `--theta`, and there is no Tmax anywhere.
- **E8. Copper loss never reaches thermal.** `[pcbqs] M`
  - Neither the circuit's copper loss (28.8 mW on the buck) nor the PDN's per-triangle DC loss.
- **E9. There is no electro-thermal loop.** `[pcbqs] M`
  - Losses are taken at 27 °C, and σ is a constant 5.8e7.
  - Fix: thermal → scale R by temperature → rerun warm, two or three passes, with no change to the engine.
- **E10. Inductor loss is DCR plus one constant core resistor.** `[pcbqs] M · partly`
  - Missing: iGSE core loss, and Rac per harmonic after the run.
- **E11. A sweep gives one thermal map.** `[pcbqs] S–M`
  - It is picked by total loss, including off-board parts.
- **E12. Vendor models come in through a narrow door.** `[pcbqs] S–M · partly`
  - `pin_order` must be D, G, S; the diode takes no `.model`; KiCad's `Sim.*` fields are ignored.
- **E13. Thermal's surroundings are one film coefficient.** `[pcbqs] M`
  - Missing: heatsinks or fixed-temperature regions, body-to-air paths, forced air and transients.
- **E14. The PDN lacks what a PI user expects.** `[pdn] M`
  - Missing: a Touchstone or SPICE export of its port matrix, time-domain droop, vendor decap models and DC-bias
    derating.
  - The near field uses one `--esr`/`--esl` for every input capacitor, unlike the PDN.
  - There is no planeless rail impedance: the PEEC route is the trial only.

**Lines and SI**
- **E15. Discontinuities.** `[lines] M · partly`
  - The scikit-rf backend has none; qucsator has nothing for 45° bends or 5-arm junctions.
  - dfsynth's KJK open end, Garg-Bahl gap and Edwards step could be ported.
- **E16. Crosstalk in time comes from a fixed 401-point linear grid.** `[si] S`
  - Long traces and fast edges read low: 3.3 % for a 5 % coupler at 1 m and 25 ps. TDR and eyes will inherit it.
- **E17. Coupled lines with a jog aren't grouped.** `[lines] S–M · partly`
  - dfsynth's edge-coupled band-pass is open on the line model.
- **E18. dfsynth calibrates only the band edges.** `[dfsynth] M`
  - The hairpin's return loss is 5.5 dB on its own lossless model, while the README still lists 16.4 dB.
  - Missing: elliptic and cross-coupled designs, combline, SIR, the tapped hairpin feed, and a `.kicad_mod` output.

**kicad2ems-fem**
- **E19. Palace eigenmode, the one use NOTES says Palace is worth.** `M`
- **E20. Palace accuracy tools are unused.** `M`

**The window and the KiCad plugin**

- **E24. Failed and stopped runs vanish, and their logs are not kept.** `[gui] M · partly`

## In good shape

- **Architecture.** Settings are declared once as `Option`, and the CLI, TOML, window and dataclass are generated from
  them. The plugin registries isolate broken plugins. The run, event and board formats are versioned, the parity and
  deviation mechanism holds across solvers, and the report rebuilds from run.json alone.
- **Line model and SI.** The 2D field solver is well checked, and its rules for when a section needs its cut are
  reasoned and measured. SI's explanations are clear.
- **mom25d.** Its layered Green's functions are tested to about 1e-5. Its mixed-precision direct solver, with LUs kept
  across the sweep, is well engineered. Its compiled BLR raises rather than returning garbage.
- **gfdtd.** One update file is shared by CuPy, Vulkan and the CPU core, and runs near this GPU's bandwidth bound
  (6–7.7 G cells/s). Its back ends are tested against NumPy.
- **pcbqs.**
  - The validation suite rests on independent references and says honestly what still differs.
  - The compiled kernels have NumPy twins held to them by tests, and the code refuses a silent GPU fallback.
  - The circuit's power books balance to 1e-8.
  - The field hand-off uses the simulated waveform, with consistent phases.
  - Thermal agrees with Elmer within 2 K and names every part it leaves out.
- **kicad2ems-fem.** Each tool's argparse parser is its engine's settings. Elmer's quirks are documented where they
  are worked around.
- **kicad2ems-ipc.** The plugin is cleanly split: a Qt-free model tested on three boards, and a stand-in for kipy.
